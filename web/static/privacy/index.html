Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 | Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and India DPDP Act 2023

🛡️ Our Privacy Pledge: getArole processes your resume and profile data solely to calculate AI match scores, suggest resume enhancements, and connect you with job opportunities. We never sell your personal information to third-party data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to deliver personalized job discovery services:

  • Account Identification: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers provided via Google Authentication or Firebase Auth.
  • Resume & Professional Profile Data: Resumes uploaded in PDF format, extracted work history, education, skills, projects, and portfolio links.
  • Job Search Preferences: Target job titles, preferred work modes (Remote, Hybrid, Onsite), target cities, and salary expectations.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address (redacted in logs), browser user-agent, error logs, and anonymized performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is processed for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Matching your candidate profile against aggregated job listings using local semantic embeddings.
  • Providing AI-assisted resume bullet enhancements and tailored executive summaries via secure subprocessor APIs (NVIDIA NIM / OpenAI).
  • Persisting your profile and application preferences in Supabase for cross-device access.
  • Monitoring platform health, preventing denial-of-service abuse, and securing API endpoints.

3. Subprocessors & Data Transfers

We partner with enterprise cloud infrastructure providers under strict Data Processing Agreements:

  • Supabase: Cloud PostgreSQL database for profile, preference, and job index persistence.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud: User authentication and token verification.
  • NVIDIA NIM / AI Inference APIs: Ephemeral processing of resume bullet suggestions (data is not retained for model training).

4. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & India DPDP Act 2023)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can inspect and export your saved profile data at any time from your Profile settings.
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your skills, experience, and preferences directly via the getArole dashboard.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can trigger immediate, permanent deletion of your profile, preferences, and uploaded resume data via DELETE /api/user/account or within the Profile menu.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information (CCPA): getArole does not sell or share consumer personal data.

5. Data Retention & Security

We apply defense-in-depth security measures including SHA-256 integrity hashing, HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, least-privilege database row-level security (RLS), and rate-limited API firewalls. User data is retained only for the duration of an active user account.
