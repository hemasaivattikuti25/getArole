Frequently Asked Questions

What makes getArole different from LinkedIn or Naukri? ▾
Unlike traditional job boards that are filled with expired listings, sponsored spam, and ghost jobs, getArole runs automated scrapers every 3 hours directly against official enterprise career portals. Every opening is 100% live, verified, and matched to your resume with an AI fit score.
Which companies does getArole scrape? ▾
getArole ingests jobs across India's top IT giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL Tech, Tech Mahindra), global tech enterprises (Google, Microsoft, Amazon), and top startups (Razorpay, Zerodha, CRED, Swiggy, Flipkart, PhonePe).
Is getArole suitable for freshers and batch 2025/2026? ▾
Yes! getArole includes dedicated filters for Internships, Graduate Trainee roles, and Entry-Level Software Engineer positions across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Remote.
How does the AI resume matching work? ▾
When you upload your resume, getArole extracts your skills and experience. It calculates a semantic fit score against every job description, showing you exactly why each role matches your profile.
