What makes getArole different from LinkedIn, Naukri, or Indeed? ▾
Unlike traditional job boards that are filled with expired listings, sponsored spam, and ghost jobs, getArole runs automated autonomous web scrapers every 3 hours directly against official enterprise career portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday). Every single opening is 100% live and verified. Then, a 70B parameter AI matches your specific resume against every opening with an explainable neural fit score.
Which Indian and global tech companies are scraped? ▾
getArole continuously ingests jobs across India's top IT giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL Tech, Tech Mahindra, LTIMindtree, Cognizant), global tech enterprises (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, Adobe), top Indian tech unicorns (Razorpay, Zerodha, CRED, Swiggy, Flipkart, PhonePe), and 200+ high-growth YC-backed startups.
Is getArole suitable for freshers and 2025/2026 graduates? ▾
Yes! getArole has dedicated filters for Internships, Graduate Trainee roles, and Entry-Level Software Engineer positions across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurgaon, and Remote. Freshers can instantly discover off-campus drives as soon as they are posted on company career pages.
How does the 70B AI resume matching work? ▾
When you upload your PDF resume, our engine extracts your core competencies, tech stack, years of experience, and project highlights. It then calculates a dense multi-dimensional semantic vector embedding against every job's full description, scoring your match between 0% and 100% and providing precise bullet points on why you are qualified.
